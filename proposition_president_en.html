1. Context & Governance Objectives

Currently, within THE FRATERNAL ORDER OF LADY EAGLES GROUP, the financial management application Treasurer Financial Record (deployed across PC, Android smartphones, and tablets) requires a secure password held exclusively by the Group Treasurer, Rachel Chua.

Lady Eagles Group members and officers have expressed a legitimate desire to consult the accounts, track the financial status of the Group (overall balance, breakdown across the 7 official families, progress of Monthly dues, Alalayang Agila welfare funds, and Community Service projects) without having the ability to modify, insert, or delete any accounting transaction.

2. Proposed Architectural Solution: Dual-Profile Access

To provide transparent financial reporting while maintaining strict data integrity, the application developer proposes introducing a two-tier partitioned access model right from the startup screen:

• Profile 1 — Treasurer (Full Administrative Access & Entry): Exclusively managed by Rachel Chua (Group Treasurer) via master credentials. Allows recording expenses (Debits), revenues (Credits), member roster administration, ZIP backup exports, and Wi-Fi / Cloud 5G synchronization.

• Profile 2 — Member (Read-Only Consultation): Open to Lady Eagles Group members and officers. Offers immediate, read-only consultation of totals, analytics, transaction history, and dues status. All creation, editing, and deletion controls are completely hidden or disabled.

4. Two Access Options for Presidential Decision

To finalize the entry procedure for the Member profile, two practical options are submitted for Group President Sharon Llegado's endorsement:

OPTION A: Direct 1-Click "Member Consultation" Button (No Password) RECOMMENDED FOR CONVENIENCE

On the main login screen, a distinct secondary button [ 👁️ Member Consultation (Read-Only) ] allows any Lady Eagles Group member to enter immediately without memorizing or typing any credential.

Key Advantages: Zero credential friction, immediate transparency during assemblies and executive meetings, seamless experience across all mobile devices.

OPTION B: Shared 4-Digit Group PIN (e.g., "2026") ENHANCED GROUP PRIVACY

Member consultation requires entering a shared 4-digit PIN code communicated exclusively to active Lady Eagles Group members (e.g., 2026 or selected by the President).

Key Advantages: Prevents external non-members from viewing group accounts if a phone or tablet is handed over. The PIN can be updated annually.

5. Accounting Safeguards & Data Integrity Protection

• Engine-level Write Protection: In Member mode, database mutation methods are completely blocked at the software engine level. No accidental record deletion or alteration is technically possible.

• Privilege Elevation Lock: Switching from Member view to Treasurer mode strictly requires Rachel Chua's private administrative password.

• Software Implementation: Programmed by application developer Eric Veyrat in accordance with Rachel Chua's accounting workflow and Lady Eagles Group governance.
