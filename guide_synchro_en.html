1. Understanding Synchronization in Plain English

Core Objective: Record an expense or income on any device (your desktop PC, your smartphone, or your wife's tablet) without EVER having to retype it somewhere else. All devices stay continuously balanced down to the exact cent.

The "Smart Merge" Guarantee (Zero Overwrites & Zero Data Loss)

A major concern in computing is that one device might accidentally overwrite or erase work done on another. In Rachel Eagles, this risk is technically impossible thanks to non-destructive smart merging:

  • If your PC contains entry A and your phone records entry B, syncing merges them into A + B. Both devices gain the full updated ledger.
  • If you capture a member's picture with your smartphone camera, the photo transfers to the PC and displays inside the official desktop directory.
  • The 7 official expenditure families always stay strictly arranged in alphabetical order.

2. Dual Synchronization Channels: Local Wi-Fi vs 5G Mobile Cloud

Mode When to use it? How it works in practice
Local Wi-Fi At home or office when PC and mobile share the same Wi-Fi router. Direct device-to-device communication on port 8085. Blazing fast (0.2s), secure, and does not require external internet.
5G Mobile Cloud On the go, during meetings, group charity events, or abroad. Phone pushes encrypted records to your secure Cloud vault (GitHub Contents API). Whenever the PC starts up, it pulls all updates.
Smart Sync (Auto) Recommended Default Tests in 800ms whether the home PC is reachable over Wi-Fi. If yes, syncs locally; if away on cellular data, routes via 5G Cloud automatically!

6. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

1. What if local Wi-Fi sync reports a connection error?
Ensure your PC and phone are connected to the same local Wi-Fi router. If you are away from home, simply switch to 5G Cloud mode with one tap.

2. How to transfer data if there is NO network at all (no Wi-Fi, no 5G)?
Navigate to PC Sync & Backup, click Download Complete Backup (.json). Copy this small file to a USB thumb drive or transfer it via cable to the PC, then click Restore / Merge. All records merge seamlessly!

3. Are member photos preserved during synchronization?
Yes, 100%. All compressed photos are embedded directly into both the JSON backups and the 5G Cloud vault.
